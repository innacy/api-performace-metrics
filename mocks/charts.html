Each chart answers one question. Hover for details, toggle what you need.

Where does it break?

Throughput (bars) and response times (lines) for each step of load. The red zone is where it got slower or started failing.

What to look for: bars stop growing while lines shoot up = the API is saturated. More load only adds waiting.

What happened over time

Response times through the whole run. Red ticks at the bottom are failed requests.

What to look for: spikes inside a stage (GC pauses, cold caches) and a slow upward drift (leaks, queue build-up).

How fast were most requests?

Hover anywhere to read “X% of requests finished within Y ms”.

What to look for: two humps = two kinds of requests (cache hit vs miss). A long tail right of p99 = some users wait much longer.

Share of each response type over time. A healthy run stays green.
