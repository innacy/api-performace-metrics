In plain words
  • Up to 50 users at once, your API stays fast (p99 under 120 ms).
  • At 100 at once, the slowest requests get 4× slower and 503 errors appear: this is the breaking point.
  • Throughput stopped growing after stage 3, so more load only adds waiting, not work done.
